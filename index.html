Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CampusFind AI

How does the AI image matching work? +
Our computer vision model extracts key visual features (color, brand, serial text, shape, texture) from uploaded photos and compares them against campus inventory in real-time.
Is my personal information visible to everyone? +
No. Your identity and contact details remain strictly private until a high-confidence match is verified by campus lost-and-found administrators or secure mutual confirmation.
What happens after I locate my item? +
You can verify ownership via unique identifiers (such as student ID, unlock passcode, or photo proof) and schedule a pickup at the designated campus Security Desk or Student Union center.
